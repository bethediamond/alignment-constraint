## Citing the complete framework v1.0.0

Use this citation when referring to the complete versioned framework archive, including the three series, proof program, empirical protocols, machine-readable files, application guidance, and specialist handoffs.

**Plain citation:**

## Citing the OP4 / Stability Assumption preprint

Use this citation when referring to the primary technical thesis, OP4, OP4d, the three identified objective-boundary strategy families, or the specification-coherence argument developed in the paper.

**Plain citation:**
Silliphant, John. (2026). *The Stability Assumption: Specification-Coherence Limits in Separable Objective Alignment* (Version 1.0.0) [Preprint]. Zenodo. [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21895992](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21895992)

## Citing the living canonical website

Use the living archive when referring to material that may have changed after v1.0.0, or when citing a specific canonical page. Include an access date.

**Plain citation:**
Silliphant, John. (2026–present). *The Alignment Constraint Framework*. [https://alignmentconstraint.org/](https://alignmentconstraint.org/) (accessed YYYY-MM-DD).

For a specific technical claim, cite the relevant canonical page in addition to the fixed DOI record when useful.
