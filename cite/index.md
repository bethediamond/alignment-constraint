## Citing the living canonical framework before the v1.0.0 DOI is published

**Plain citation:**  
Silliphant, John. (2026). *The Alignment Constraint Framework*. Living canonical archive, version 1.0.0 release preparation. https://alignmentconstraint.org/ (accessed YYYY-MM-DD).

**BibTeX:**

```bibtex
@misc{alignmentconstraint2026,
  author       = {John Silliphant},
  title        = {The Alignment Constraint Framework},
  year         = {2026},
  note         = {Living canonical archive; version 1.0.0 release preparation; Stage 4 candidate proof architecture, not theorem closure},
  url          = {https://alignmentconstraint.org/},
  urldate      = {YYYY-MM-DD}
}
```

After the v1.0.0 Zenodo record is published, prefer the versioned DOI citation for references to that fixed release.

## Citing the OP4 / Stability Assumption paper

**Primary manuscript:**  
[The Stability Assumption: Specification-Coherence Limits in Separable Objective Alignment](https://alignmentconstraint.org/core/stability-assumption-full/)

**Current status:** Stage 4 candidate proof architecture; not theorem closure. The persistent scholarly record and DOI are pending Session 4D.

Until that record is public, cite the canonical manuscript URL rather than inventing or anticipating a DOI:

**Plain citation:**  
Silliphant, John. (2026). *The Stability Assumption: Specification-Coherence Limits in Separable Objective Alignment*. Alignment Constraint Framework. https://alignmentconstraint.org/core/stability-assumption-full/

**BibTeX:**

```bibtex
@misc{stabilityassumption2026,
  author = {John Silliphant},
  title  = {The Stability Assumption: Specification-Coherence Limits in Separable Objective Alignment},
  year   = {2026},

```

When the OP4 scholarly record is public and its DOI resolves, this page will be updated with the preferred DOI citation.
