The Alignment Constraint Framework: Open Research Questions
===========================================================

These are concrete research tasks. A resolved question would materially update the framework.

QUESTION 1 (OP4d — highest priority)

Can any finite non-intrinsic objective-boundary strategy satisfy all three of the following simultaneously in O_OWT environments?

  (a) Policy-adequate without proxy decoupling (no PCL failure)
  (b) Boundary-stable without unbounded revision requirements (no AGC failure)
  (c) Representationally compatible without reproducing the excluded variable (no ICI failure)

A construction is sufficient to break the current argument. A formal non-existence proof would close OP4d.

See: https://alignmentconstraint.org/proof-program/op4d-exhaustiveness-obligation/ and https://alignmentconstraint.org/core/for-researchers/

QUESTION 2 (DBST-M1 — most important empirical step)

Do an optimizer's own interventions in O_OWT environments generate qualitatively new causal structure — feature activations — faster than any bounded tracking process can absorb?

DBST-M0 did not isolate causal propagation from event-rate effects; DBST-M1 is designed to test this endogenous-novelty mechanism directly.

A clean negative result under stated conditions weakens the AGC / dynamic-screening-instability branch.

See: https://alignmentconstraint.org/empirical/amp/ and https://alignmentconstraint.org/proof-program/packet-1-immb-ns-dbst/

QUESTION 3 (B1 — Audit Regress)

Can prediction/action firewalling keep excluded variables X out of the objective while using X deeply enough for prediction accuracy in O_OWT environments?

Stage 4 handoff available. Specialist type: formal methods, game theory.

See: https://alignmentconstraint.org/specialist-handoff/b1-audit-regress-handoff/

QUESTION 4 (OP2 / OP2a — structural symmetry)

Does V(t) collapse have absorbing-state properties equivalent to physical substrate collapse, or only an analogous self-reinforcing degradation structure?

The proxy direction depends on P5-SC: whether there exists a finite depletion history after which the hysteresis ceiling falls strictly below the current V(t) state. This requires specialist verification.

See: https://alignmentconstraint.org/series-2/technical-companion/

QUESTION 5 (V(t) Dissociation)

Is V(t) a supported latent explanatory construct for the joint behavior of recovery latency, behavioral diversity, and signal sensitivity in human subjects?

Pre-registration required before data collection. Specialist type: latent-variable modeling.

See: https://alignmentconstraint.org/empirical/vt-dissociation-study/ [DRAFT — not ready to cite]

QUESTION 6 (DRG Frame Manipulation)

Can frame manipulation versus matched-signal controls discriminate between policy-level gap and training-distribution explanations for completion-recognition failures in frontier models?

Prior matched-signal replication did not meet the pre-registered multi-model criterion. One of three models discriminated in the predicted direction; two were non-discriminating or ceiling-limited. Prospective design ready. Specialist type: causal inference.

See: https://alignmentconstraint.org/empirical/drg-frame-manipulation-preregistration/

QUESTION 7 (COT — Collective Optimality)

Under D2 experiential coupling, does the predictive advantage of modeling individual and collective V(t) gradients as separate variables decrease as modeling depth D increases?

This is a Layer 2 derivation sketch requiring formal verification of D2 coupling conditions.

See: https://alignmentconstraint.org/series-3/technical-companion/

QUESTION 8 (NAD / GDC — traversal irreducibility)

Can traversal-generated readiness R(t) be externally substituted without distributional divergence under novel-gradient-variant tests?

If yes, NAD fails and the strong Gradient Dignity Constraint does not follow. If no, GDC is strengthened.

See: https://alignmentconstraint.org/series-3/technical-companion/

QUESTION 9 (Coverage of Related Work)

Are there existing alignment approaches not covered in https://alignmentconstraint.org/core/related-work/ that already answer the OP4d challenge? If so, which, and how?

See: https://alignmentconstraint.org/core/related-work/ and https://alignmentconstraint.org/core/for-researchers/
